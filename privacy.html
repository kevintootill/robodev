Privacy & cookies

Updated 5 October 2026.

Optional analytics

With your permission, RoboDev uses Google Analytics 4 to measure page visits, traffic sources and scrolling. Google may process device and browser information, approximate location and cookie identifiers. We do not send your name, email address or code to Analytics. Advertising personalisation and Google signals are disabled in our website tag.

The Analytics tag loads only after you select “Accept analytics”. If you reject, the site works without Analytics. Your choice is saved in your browser's local storage. Accepting allows Google Analytics to set first-party cookies such as _ga, which may last up to two years.

lets you accept or withdraw permission. Rejecting removes accessible Analytics cookies and stops further collection. It does not delete previously collected data.

Hosting

SiteGround hosts this website and may keep server logs containing IP addresses, request times, requested pages and browser information for hosting, security and troubleshooting.

Contact enquiries

The contact form sends your name, email address and message to kevintootill@hotmail.com so Kevin Tootill can reply. Messages are processed by SiteGround's mail service and the destination email provider. Please do not send passwords or sensitive personal information. We keep enquiries as needed to respond and manage the conversation.

The form uses an essential session cookie to prevent forged submissions. Temporary spam controls store a hashed IP address and recent submission timestamps on the server. Contact fields are not sent to Google Analytics.

Google's processing

Google processes Analytics data under its own terms and policies. See Google's privacy policy and how Google uses information from partner sites. Standard Analytics user and event data retention is currently set to the property's default two months; aggregated reports may be retained longer.
